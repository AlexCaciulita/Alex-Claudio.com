A few details

Before we
begin.

Will you both photograph our wedding?

Yes. Every collection includes both of us for the full six, eight, or ten hours of wedding coverage. Having two photographers lets us cover separate preparations and capture different perspectives on the ceremony and celebration.

How much coverage do we need?

Six hours suits a compact timeline. Eight hours gives more room for preparations, portraits, and the reception. Ten hours allows for a longer celebration or more travel between locations. We'll map out the schedule together before you choose.

Which collections include a leather album?

Signature includes a 10 × 10-inch leather album with 15 spreads / 30 pages. Heirloom includes a 12 × 12-inch leather album with 20 spreads / 40 pages. Both include custom design and your approval before printing. An album can also be quoted for Essential.

Is an engagement session included?

Heirloom includes a 60-minute engagement session. It's a chance to spend time together and feel more comfortable in front of the camera before your wedding. Engagement sessions can be added to Essential or Signature; we'll include the cost and location details in your proposal.

Can we add coverage or plan a destination wedding?

Yes. Tell us about additional events, locations, or hours you'd like covered. We'll prepare a proposal that specifies the photographers, travel, and coverage for each part of your celebration.

How do we reserve our date?

Send an inquiry with your date and venue. We'll confirm availability, discuss your plans, and review your collection together. Your proposal will include the agreement, payment schedule, and gallery delivery timeline.
