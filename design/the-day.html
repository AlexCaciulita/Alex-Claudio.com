I Getting ready

Half of your wedding happens behind your back.

So there are two of us. While you get ready in separate rooms, one of us stays with each of you. At the ceremony, one of us watches you and the other watches everyone watching you.

Camera one

Camera two

II The ceremony

One of us faces you. The other faces everyone who came.

You get your own faces, and theirs: your parents, your oldest friends, and a few people you didn’t know were crying.

III Golden hour

When the light turns, we’ll borrow you for a few minutes.

You’ll be back with your guests before your drink gets warm.

IV After dark

f/22

Don’t send us home at the first dance.

You’ll barely remember the end of the night. You were in the middle of it. If the send-off matters to you, book enough hours to reach it.

V A few weeks later

Every photograph you receive was chosen by one of us.

We go through your whole day frame by frame, keep the ones worth keeping, and edit each of them ourselves.
