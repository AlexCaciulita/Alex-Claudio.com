V How it works

From your first letter to your gallery.

  1. 1

    Write to me

    Send the package from this page, or just your date. I answer every inquiry myself, within a few hours.

  2. 2

    A call

    We talk about where the day is, who’ll be there, and what you most want photographed.

  3. 3

    Your proposal

    Your package with tax and travel added, the agreement, and the payment schedule. Signing it and paying a 30% retainer reserves your date.

  4. 4

    The engagement session

    If you’ve added one, somewhere you like, before the wedding.

  5. 5

    The timeline

    We work out the order of the day together, so everything you want photographed fits inside your hours.

  6. 6

    The wedding

    I photograph the day as it happens. When the light turns, I’ll borrow you for a few minutes.

  7. 7

    Your gallery

    Within six weeks of the wedding, your photographs arrive in a private online gallery, every one edited by me. If you’ve chosen an album, I design it and you approve the layout before it prints.

VI Good to know

A few questions.

Will you photograph the wedding yourself?

Yes, and I edit every photograph myself. From eight hours you can add a second photographer for $1,000, so each of you has a photographer while you get ready.

How many hours do we need?

Name the first moment you want photographed and the last one you’d hate to miss, then count the hours between them. Leave room for dinner or speeches to run late. The Journal has a longer guide to choosing hours.

What isn’t in the price?

Tax, and travel if your wedding needs it. Both are listed in your proposal before you book.

Do you photograph weddings outside Seattle?

Yes, across the Pacific Northwest and in Europe, including elopements. Tell me where and I’ll add travel to your proposal.

Can you cover a rehearsal dinner or a second day?

Yes, priced separately from the wedding day. Add it in the “Anything else?” box above and I’ll include it in your proposal.

When do we get our photographs?

Your gallery arrives within six weeks of the wedding. If you’ve chosen an album, it goes to print once you’ve approved the layout.

How do we reserve our date?

Send me your package below with your date and venue. I’ll confirm I’m free, we’ll talk through your plans, and I’ll send a proposal. Your date is reserved once you sign the agreement and pay a 30% retainer.
